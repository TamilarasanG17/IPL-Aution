Auction Instructions

1. How the Auction Works

  1. Open the Players List page. It shows all players of the current stage (pool) with base price and previous team.
  2. Click Start to begin that stage. Click Skip to jump to the next stage.
  3. On the auction screen, players appear in random order one by one.
  4. Press S to open the player card, raise the bid with + / -, then press the team key to sell, or U for unsold.
  5. Press Enter to move to the next player.
  6. After all stages, press F and click Finish to see the top teams by points.

3. Keyboard Controls (Auction Screen)

KeyAction
SShow / hide the player card (current bid, previous team). Open this first before bidding.
+Increase the current bid
-Reduce the current bid
Enter / →Next player (stops the anthem, hides the SOLD / UNSOLD image)
Backspace / ←Previous player
UMark player UNSOLD
FShow the Finish button (top-right)
CtrlReset all bids (danger)
ShiftReset all team budgets to 100 Cr and points to 0 (danger)
Warning: Ctrl and Shift run instantly with no confirmation. Pressing + with the main keyboard row needs Shift, which can reset every team's budget. Use the numpad + during a live auction.

6. Team Rules

  • Every team starts with a purse of 100 Crore.
  • A team cannot bid more than its remaining purse.
  • Maximum 11 players per team.
  • Maximum 1 Wicket Keeper.
  • Maximum 5 Batsmen + Wicket Keepers + All-Rounders combined.
  • Maximum 3 Pace Bowlers.
  • Maximum 1 Spin Bowler.
  • Maximum 4 Foreign players.
  • If a team breaks a rule, the purchase is rejected (see the browser console for the reason).

7. Finishing & Results

  1. Press F on the auction screen. The Finish button appears at the top-right.
  2. Click Finish. Teams are ranked by total player points.
  3. The top 10 teams are shown on the results page.
Player points are hidden during the auction and are only used for the final ranking.

8. Quick Tips

  • Use a keyboard. The auction screen is fully keyboard controlled.
  • Keep the sound on so team anthems play. Browsers may need one click on the page first.
  • Before starting a new auction, press Shift once to reset budgets, and Ctrl to reset bids.
  • Keep the backend server running, because players and teams are loaded from /api.
